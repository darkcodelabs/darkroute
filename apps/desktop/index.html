DarkRoute ALPR camera archive and map

Browse community-mapped automated license plate readers, also known as ALPR or ANPR cameras. DarkRoute's archive uses OpenStreetMap contributions and provides camera coverage, source records, and a public API.

These are mapped camera locations, not records of license plates or vehicle sightings. Coverage may be incomplete or out of date.

Read the ALPR camera guide for camera types, Flock readers, routing, and privacy limits.
