DarkRoute: ALPR camera map and license plate reader alerts

DarkRoute helps you understand where automated license plate readers are mapped. Explore community-mapped ALPR and ANPR cameras, receive nearby camera alerts, and compare driving routes around known readers.

Camera locations come from OpenStreetMap contributors. Coverage can be incomplete or out of date; an empty map does not mean there are no cameras.

No account or analytics SDK is required. The interactive app uses JavaScript; the guide below works without it.
